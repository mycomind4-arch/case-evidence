## Rules

- One testable proposition per claim ID.
- Preserve exact quoted wording separately from your neutral paraphrase.
- A claim's popularity, plausibility, or institutional source does not establish its truth.
- Do not treat absence from searched sources as proof of nonexistence; record search scope.
- Assign confidence to the precise wording, not to a broader narrative.
- When a claim changes, retain its ID if meaning is stable and document the change; create a new ID if its meaning changes materially.
