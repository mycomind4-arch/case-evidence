## Rules

- Shared timing, visual similarity, repeated phrases, and graph proximity generate leads; they do not establish causation or coordination.
- Show source lineage and missing links.
- Keep “claim repeated in both dossiers” separate from “the events are causally connected.”
- Do not remove a plausible lead solely because it is unconventional; label it accurately and specify a test.
