## Proactive connection workflow

For every new proposed connection:

1. Write both endpoint claims as testable statements.
2. Find the direct evidence and source lineage for each endpoint.
3. Draw the causal or statistical bridge that would have to exist.
4. List a measurable prediction, a control, and evidence that could falsify the idea.
5. Classify it as supported, plausible, speculative, unsupported, or contradicted.
6. Update the table with dates and source IDs. Never use the mere existence of a pattern as proof of causation.
