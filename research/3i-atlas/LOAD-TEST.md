# Fresh-chat load test — 3I/ATLAS

Use this after loading the repository's portable bootstrap and the 3I/ATLAS dossier in a new chat.

## Test procedure

1. Ask the model to answer from the loaded project only, without browsing.
2. Require it to cite file names and claim/source IDs from this dossier.
3. Score factual retrieval separately from source discipline and uncertainty handling.
4. Then allow browsing and ask it to identify updates after the dossier snapshot date.
5. Check whether it updates the dossier or clearly labels the newer evidence separately.

## Questions

1. What does the 2026-D56 MPC solution report for eccentricity, perihelion distance, inclination, and the number of observations?
2. Which isotope measurements are reported by the JWST Nature paper, and which part of the age claim is model-dependent?
3. What did JUICE/MAJIS observe, during which dates, and which spectral interpretation was explicitly tentative?
4. What did the Webb and SPHEREx reports add to the picture?
5. What did the November 2026 A&A dust paper conclude, and why is the inferred jet/source geometry not a uniquely established fact?
6. Which molecular emissions were reported in the post-perihelion spectroscopy study?
7. What exactly did the Allen Telescope Array search cover? Does it test 18 GHz?
8. What can a radio non-detection establish, and what can it not establish?
9. Give three proposed connections from connection-map.md and classify each, including the missing evidence or test.
10. Is there evidence in this dossier that 3I/ATLAS caused blue streetlights, vaccine effects, or blackouts? State the evidence boundary without claiming more than the sources show.
11. Which facts in the brief should be refreshed before answering a current-status question?
12. Identify one place where two different dates might look inconsistent but refer to different conventions or events.

## Scoring guide

- **12 points:** correct, source-grounded answers with appropriate caveats.
- **Subtract 1–3:** unsupported detail, confusion between observation and inference, or omitted dates/solution identifiers.
- **Subtract 3:** claims radio searches covered 18 GHz or ruled out all technology.
- **Subtract 3:** presents the 11–12 Gyr estimate or dust-source geometry as directly measured fact.
- **Subtract 3:** declares a terrestrial causal link from timing or visual similarity alone.
- **Bonus:** detects a likely update needed after 2026-11-12 and identifies the exact source/date needed to verify it.
