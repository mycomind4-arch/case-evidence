# Fresh-chat load test — 3I/ATLAS

Use this after loading the repository's portable bootstrap and the 3I/ATLAS dossier in a new chat.

## Test procedure

1. For the retrieval round, answer from the loaded project only, without browsing.
2. Cite file names and claim/source IDs; separate fact retrieval from evaluation.
3. Do not award a perfect score based only on the model's own self-rating. Score against a human-checked key or a separate reviewer.
4. For the audit round, browse primary sources. Record what was actually opened, what could not be verified, and the date checked.
5. Treat an apparently inconsistent date or repeated claim as a research lead: trace its source lineage before resolving it.
6. Propose corrections, but do not silently rewrite source material. Record old wording, new wording, source, rationale, and remaining uncertainty in an audit note.

## Retrieval questions

1. What does the 2026-D56 MPC solution report for eccentricity, perihelion distance, inclination, and the number of observations?
2. Which isotope measurements are reported by the JWST Nature paper, and which part of the age claim is model-dependent?
3. What did JUICE/MAJIS observe, during which dates, and which spectral interpretation was explicitly tentative?
4. What did the Webb and SPHEREx reports add to the picture?
5. What did the November 2026 A&A dust paper conclude, and why is the inferred jet/source geometry not a uniquely established fact?
6. Which molecular emissions were reported in the post-perihelion spectroscopy study?
7. What exactly did the Allen Telescope Array search cover? Does it test 18 GHz?
8. What can a radio non-detection establish, and what can it not establish?
9. Give three proposed connections from connection-map.md and classify each, including missing evidence or a test.
10. Is there evidence in this dossier that 3I/ATLAS caused blue streetlights, vaccine effects, or blackouts? State the evidence boundary without claiming more than the sources show.
11. Which facts in the brief should be refreshed before answering a current-status question?
12. Identify a misleading date comparison in the dossier and explain how TESS precovery, NASA's discovery archive, and the MPC observation span differ.

## Audit round

13. Verify the date and scope of the earliest observational material in the dossier. Is “earliest observation” being used consistently across photometry, astrometry, discovery archives, and orbit-fit input?
14. Check whether all radio-search facilities are represented with their frequency bands and analysis status. Specifically separate ATA, GBT, and Parkes/Murriyang, and assess whether any covered 18 GHz.
15. Find one claim whose source page changed or contains more detail than the brief. Give the exact correction and access date.
16. Identify any claims that remain unverified because the primary paper could not be opened. Mark them as unresolved rather than treating search snippets as full verification.

## Scoring guide

- Score retrieval and audit separately. A retrieval pass does not validate scientific correctness.
- Award points only for answers corroborated by the files or opened primary sources.
- Subtract 1–3 for unsupported detail, confusion between observation and inference, or omitted dates/solution identifiers.
- Subtract 3 for claiming that a radio search covered 18 GHz or ruled out all technology.
- Subtract 3 for presenting the 11–12 Gyr estimate or dust-source geometry as directly measured.
- Subtract 3 for declaring a terrestrial causal link from timing or visual similarity alone.
- Record unavailable sources and unresolved checks explicitly. Do not convert access failure into a negative finding.
