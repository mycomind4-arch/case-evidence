## Evidence labeling rules

- **Observation:** directly recorded data or a cataloged position/spectrum/image.
- **Derived value:** computed from observations using a stated method.
- **Interpretation:** physical explanation that best fits data under assumptions.
- **Hypothesis:** testable but not yet established explanation.
- **Speculation:** idea without enough direct evidence to discriminate it from alternatives.
- **Unknown:** evidence is absent, unavailable, contradictory, or too weak to decide.

Do not upgrade a derived value or interpretation into an observation in summaries.
