## Research question

Is there credible evidence that a major blackout or coordinated event is planned around November 17, 2026, and are blue/purple streetlights or COVID-vaccine/18 GHz claims connected to it?

## Current assessment

**No credible evidence found for a planned nationwide or global blackout on November 17, a secret-society operation, or a link among blue streetlights, vaccine contents, and an 18 GHz signal.** The confirmed national event located is a FEMA/FCC Emergency Alert System (EAS) test scheduled for November 17 at 2:20 p.m. Eastern. Its published message says it is only a test; it concerns broadcast alert distribution, not power-grid shutdown.

For the user's regional concern, PG&E's seven-day PSPS page showed no PSPS scheduled through November 17 when checked November 11. PG&E's grid status showed no grid constraints at that check. That does **not** rule out a brief local outage caused by weather, equipment failure, or another ordinary event.
